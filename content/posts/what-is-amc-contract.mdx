An annual maintenance contract (AMC) is an agreement between a service business and a customer that covers planned maintenance over a defined period.

## Why AMCs matter

AMCs turn one-off jobs into a more dependable relationship. Customers get confidence that their equipment is looked after, while service teams gain a clearer view of upcoming work and revenue.

A useful AMC should make three things obvious:

- What equipment or services are covered
- How often maintenance visits happen
- What is included, excluded, or billed separately

## What to include in an AMC

Start with a plain-language scope of work. Include visit frequency, response expectations, pricing, renewal terms, and the process for requesting additional work. Clear terms prevent avoidable confusion for both sides.

The operational details matter just as much. Keep customer contacts, contract dates, technician assignments, visit notes, and invoices connected in one place so the team can act on the agreement—not just store it.

## Make the contract easy to manage

A well-run AMC process gives your team reminders before renewals, visibility into overdue visits, and a consistent record of every service interaction. That is how recurring work becomes a reliable system instead of a spreadsheet someone has to remember to update.
