Missed visits cost more than a slot on the calendar. They interrupt customer trust, create extra coordination, and make an already busy team work twice.

The good news is that most missed visits are preventable with a few consistent habits.

## 1. Confirm before the day

Send a short confirmation with the date, arrival window, technician name, and anything the customer needs to prepare. Make confirmations part of the workflow rather than relying on memory.

## 2. Keep customer details current

A technician should not have to hunt for the right contact, address, access instructions, or equipment history. Keep these details together and make updates easy after every visit.

## 3. Use reminders for recurring work

Recurring maintenance is easy to lose sight of when it lives in a calendar without context. Set reminders for upcoming visits and renewals early enough to give your team time to schedule well.

## 4. Close the loop after every job

A simple completion note—what was checked, what changed, and what comes next—helps the office team spot follow-up work before it becomes another missed commitment.

## 5. Review the exceptions

Look at the visits that were rescheduled, delayed, or missed. Patterns usually point to a fix: unrealistic travel windows, incomplete customer information, or reminders sent too late.

Reliable service is rarely about one dramatic change. It comes from making the right small action easy to repeat.
