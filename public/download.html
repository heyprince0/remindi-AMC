One-Click Install Works on Android & Desktop No App Store Needed

Already installed, or use your browser menu → "Install App" / "Add to Home Screen".

Step by Step

Install in Under 30 Seconds

Choose your device and follow three simple steps.

1
🌐

Open Chrome

On your Android phone, open Google Chrome and go to app.remindi.online. Sign in or create a free account.

2
⋮

Tap the Menu

Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner. Look for "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".

3
✅

Confirm & Done

Tap Install or Add. Remindi will appear on your home screen — tap to open it anytime like a native app.

1
🌐

Open Safari

On your iPhone or iPad, open Safari and go to app.remindi.online. Log in or sign up for free. (iOS requires Safari for PWA install.)

2
📤

Tap Share

Tap the Share icon (box with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of Safari. Scroll down in the share sheet to find more options.

3
➕

Add to Home Screen

Tap "Add to Home Screen" and then tap Add in the top-right. Remindi is now on your iPhone just like a real app.

1
🖥️

Open Chrome or Edge

On Windows or Mac, open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and go to app.remindi.online. Sign in to your account.

2
📥

Click Install Icon

Look for a computer icon with a down arrow (⬇) in the address bar on the right side. Click it to see the install prompt.

3
🚀

Install & Launch

Click Install. Remindi opens in its own window, lives in your taskbar or Dock, and sends desktop notifications for due AMCs.

All Platforms

Detailed Steps for Every Device

Clear instructions for Android, iPhone, Windows, and Mac.

🤖

Android

Works on all Android devices with Chrome 76+. Supports home screen install, offline access, and push notifications.

  • Open Chrome → go to app.remindi.online
  • Tap ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen"
  • Tap Install on the popup → done
🍎

iPhone & iPad (iOS)

iOS 16.4+ supports full PWA with push notifications. Must use Safari — Chrome on iOS does not support home screen install.

  • Open Safari → app.remindi.online
  • Tap Share button (box with up arrow)
  • Tap "Add to Home Screen" → Add
🪟

Windows PC

Install via Chrome or Edge on Windows 10/11. Runs as a standalone window with taskbar pinning and desktop notifications.

  • Open Chrome or Edge → app.remindi.online
  • Click the install icon (⬇) in the address bar
  • Click Install → Remindi opens in its own window
🍏

Mac

Works on macOS 10.15+ via Chrome or Edge. After install, Remindi appears in your Launchpad and Dock like a native app.

  • Open Chrome or Edge → app.remindi.online
  • Click the ⬇ icon in the address bar
  • Click Install → pin to Dock for quick access
